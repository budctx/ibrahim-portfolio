Operations tool · Event scoring

SKY Marathon

From event results to participant standings: a focused workflow for entry, review, saving and correction.

My role

Scoring UX direction, iterative refinement and AI-assisted development

The problem

Tournaments, weekly activities, daily events and Stage sessions use different point rules. The scoring experience needed to accommodate those differences and make the resulting points clear before confirmation.

Design decisions

One member identity

Participant names were removed from entry, display and export to match the operating need. Discord ID is the stable link between results; administrator names remain for accountability.

Put errors where they occur

Feedback attaches to the invalid field and focuses the first error. A winner/participant conflict blocks review; repeats within the participant list are deduplicated with notice.

Show completion and the next step

Completion reports member count and processed points with a link to standings. Editing a new draft clears the previous completion message so the states do not get confused.

Context

I directed the scoring experience for the SKY community and refined the forms, leaderboard and member lookup with AI-assisted development. The tool connects scheduled events with scoring records and aggregates each member’s results by Discord ID.

The experience

Entry and commitment are separate. The organizer reviews the event, members and points before confirming the save. A correction also previews the old result, new result and effect on the member total.

Outcome and learning

The tool is deployed. Recorded reviews cover all four scoring categories, duplicates, review-before-save, corrections and exports with isolated test data. Production checks included member lookup, exports and a correction preview that was cancelled. The lesson was that operational confidence depends on clarity before and after an action. Shorter copy helps when it removes repetition while preserving the consequences of a decision.
