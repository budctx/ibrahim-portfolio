Digital professional identity

ATHAR

A professional identity that brings profile, contact and sharing into one destination.

My role

Product direction, UX and AI-assisted development

The problem

A professional introduction often spreads across a CV, a portfolio and several social profiles. The design focus was to bring those pieces into a clear identity and a direct path to contact.

Design decisions

Let content set the hierarchy

Identity and role lead, followed by skills, links and contact actions. The card introduces its owner before asking visitors to choose a destination.

Respect each language

I reviewed field direction, labels and light/dark consistency. Links and numbers retain their reading direction inside the Arabic interface.

Keep dismissal predictable

Dialogs share focus containment, Escape dismissal and return focus to the opener. Unsaved-change warnings remain where edits could be lost.

Context

ATHAR is a personal digital business-card product. I directed the creation and sharing experience, reviewed the bilingual interface and used AI tools to support development. The public card stays focused on the identity; owner controls belong in a separate management flow.

The experience

The journey moves from building an identity to sharing its address. Recipients open the card through a link, QR code or compatible NFC card/device without installing an app to view it.

Outcome and learning

ATHAR is available as a live product. Its review records document larger text and interaction targets, keyboard behavior in dialogs and distinct failed-versus-empty data states. The lesson was that collecting information is only the start. Hierarchy, content limits and direct access determine whether the card is useful during an introduction.

The image is Ibrahim’s card already shown in this portfolio, rather than the current marketing homepage. Review records support the interaction changes; no measured user outcomes are claimed.
