IAU Student App

A dedicated student-app concept that organizes scattered academic information and service access around the student’s day.

My role

Concept design, interactive prototyping, stakeholder iteration and developer handoff

The problem

The services existed, but the experience was fragmented. Student information and access paths were distributed across Student Services, Blackboard, the personal profile and other university areas. The brief called for a dedicated student-app concept.

Design decisions

Priorities before volume

The home view surfaces academic status, alerts, the next class and the next task. Detail belongs in its own flow, keeping immediate priorities visible.

Keep shortcuts bounded

The prototype allows four service shortcuts and three important links. Bounded personalization gives students choice without overwhelming the home view.

Reveal details in context

Lecturer details open from the class card; academic records use search and term selection. Detail follows the student’s question rather than appearing all at once.

Context

I developed the concept and interactive prototype through my work in IAU’s Applications Department. I reviewed iterations with my manager, incorporated requested changes and additions, and shared a live preview for progress reviews. I then handed the revised prototype to developers, who began implementation.

The experience

I organized the concept around everyday questions: what needs attention, where is the next class, and how can a student reach the relevant service or academic information? The prototype makes those paths tangible before production integrations.

Outcome and learning

The deliverable is a reviewed, iterated prototype handed to engineering. It demonstrates a unified experience direction, rather than a launched university application or completed system integrations. The takeaway was the value of discussing behavior and sequence through a working prototype. Consistent labels, data and edge states still need attention as the concept moves into implementation.

Images come from the handed-off prototype and show service selection and the schedule without a student identity or grades. Stakeholder review and handoff reflect the owner’s account. No numerical usage outcomes or compliance certification are claimed.
